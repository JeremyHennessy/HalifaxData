# Historical municipal capital-assets explorer

This optional collection is the independently reviewed factual projection of two tables in the 2021–2022 Municipal Statistics Annual Report. It is separate from the existing municipal operating-finance collection, with no assumed alias or exact-ID crosswalk.

The public source has 49 municipality rows plus three overlapping published aggregates. Search defaults to municipality rows; selecting published totals switches to those three rows rather than mixing them into a sum. There are 468 original slots: 459 reported numbers and nine Mahone Bay excluded-reporting placeholders. Those raw zeros remain inspectable, but their normalized values are null, not zero assets.

Currency and scale are not stated in the table headings. The app displays unscaled source numbers without dollar symbols or inferred CAD. Stocks, additions, disposals and amortization retain their distinct meanings and dates. The town table's repeated 2021 date in its second accumulated-amortization header remains a printed conflict, with normalized date null. Historical asset records do not establish current condition, cash payments, vendor awards or completed projects.

The publisher pins the exact accepted data hash; runtime admission also validates shapes, unique identities, stock/movement dimensions, source locators, placeholder handling, unknown units and bounded counts. A changed snapshot cannot become accepted merely by rehashing its receipt. Original sources and detailed records remain accessible through per-row disclosures.

The existing coverage layer adds one reviewed collection only with a matching accepted receipt. If this optional receipt cannot be verified at runtime, the collection is excluded from the verified count and a source-page warning explains its unavailability; other existing collections remain usable. Dataset load failure is confined to the capital explorer and shows no values. This does not relax admission or imply unavailable records are zero.

No original inventory rows, other datasets, source receipts, findings or financial totals are modified. Hosted acceptance covers mobile readability, placeholders, date conflict, aggregate separation, empty search, and isolated data/receipt failures. Local tests do not establish successful production deployment.
