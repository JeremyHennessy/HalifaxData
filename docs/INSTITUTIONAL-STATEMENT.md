# Institutional accounts are not the payer’s cash ledger

The NSCC projection preserves 18 rows and three source columns from one consolidated statement page: institutional budget, audited current-year actual and prior-year comparative as presented. There are 54 cells, 52 numeric values and two source dashes.

The College and Foundation perimeter, inter-company eliminations and government-contribution recognition policy matter. Recognized grant revenue is not necessarily same-year cash receipt. This release does not subtract a provincial program forecast from the institutional grant line or claim a funding shortfall.

Six accumulated-surplus cells are point-in-time stocks with opening or closing dates. The other 48 cells are flow observations with fiscal-period boundaries. An explicit reviewed label-to-role mapping prevents a stock from being reclassified as an expense component. Components, reported subtotals and annual-result balances overlap and are not universally additive.

CAD is a stated contextual inference, not a literal currency code in the source. Public records retain source-dollar strings, original tokens, source columns, aggregation roles, dates, reporting perimeter and exact PDF-page locators. Only aggregate institutional facts and concise accounting context are projected; no personal salary, student or donor records, signatures, audit-opinion text or whole PDF pages are republished.
