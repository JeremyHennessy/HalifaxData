# Explain a difference rather than count an accusation

The accepted NSCC case contains 46 source observations and 14 explicit equations across two fiscal years. It explains why the annual accounting result differs from the change in cash. Every equation retains ordered input-record IDs, an output-record ID, exact source values, the calculation and its residual.

The app replays only the reviewed seven-equation graph for each year. It rejects duplicate inputs, cross-year inputs, relabeled stock/flow records, altered outputs and wrong annual-result links. Annual results are linked to exact institutional-statement IDs with compatible fiscal periods and stages. It does not turn general mixed stock/flow addition into an allowed operation.

Zero residual establishes internal published arithmetic, not the underlying bank transactions, fraud controls, business purpose or appropriateness of every expenditure. It does not reconcile the provincial departmental forecast or identify vendors. The College and Foundation remain consolidated; prior-year amounts are comparative as presented.

The brief uses ordinary language. Detailed views expose all inputs and outputs; original-evidence views expose dates, measure types, page locators, hashes and a downloadable public equation graph. Current code-release/cache clocks remain separate from source dates.
