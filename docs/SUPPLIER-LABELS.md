# Context-reviewed supplier labels

A separately reviewed extension adds 71 exact business-role supplier labels to 4,658 source observations. It joins only on the exact observation identifier and original source-row identifier, with both collections bound to the same capture manifest and ordered source digest.

This is not corporate identity resolution. Different spellings remain separate. Legal registration, beneficial ownership, aliases, corporate groups and award-to-payment relationships have not been established. Appearance in this subset says nothing about supplier performance, risk or misconduct.

Rows outside this reviewed subset remain visible in the full tender explorer. Their labels are outside the reviewed subset, not suspicious, absent or automatically private. Searching a supplier searches only the reviewed subset; therefore a zero result does not prove that the supplier has no public contracts.

Original descriptions remain withheld. Reported amounts retain unknown currency, tax and unit/total semantics; no supplier monetary totals are generated.

The supplier receipt binds the exact extension bytes and capture context. Runtime and build checks reject missing or duplicate observation identities, source-row mismatches, extra fields, changed capture context and claims of verified legal identity. Full source attribution and the Open Government Licence – Nova Scotia are retained.
