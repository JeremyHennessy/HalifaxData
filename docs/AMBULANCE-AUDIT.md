# Ground ambulance contract audit evidence

This is one grouped collection with two separately admitted projections from a historical Auditor General report. It is not a new allegation or a live service dashboard.

- Compensation: 24 source cells (16 monetary numbers, five rates, two N/A and one blank), three described contract events, two exact historical recommendation connections and departmental responses.
- Optional service context: six category standards, two July 2022 unaudited consolidated response results and 12 hospital rows plus two overlapping aggregates.
- The original contract, amendments, bank records and invoices have not been independently acquired. Monetary values retain the source's rounded-million precision; Canadian dollars are contextual inference.
- Financial fiscal-year labels and service calendar periods do not establish a matched-period or causal spending comparison. No per-dollar ratios, new totals or inferred exact successful-offload counts are produced.
- Not-completed status is historical as of October 10, 2025 and carries no assurance under the follow-up report. Department responses are not independent confirmation of later completion.
- Missing optional service evidence leaves compensation usable with an explicit warning. Missing compensation admission removes this collection from runtime verified coverage and hides its amounts.
