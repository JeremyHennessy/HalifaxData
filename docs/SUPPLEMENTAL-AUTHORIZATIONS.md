# Authorization is a distinct evidence stage

Two official orders provide 23 supplemental-appropriation clauses and 46 numerical Schedule A cells. Their fiscal years remain separate: the source totals are 523,086,000 for 2025–26 and 249,635,000 for 2026–27. The app does not add them into a cross-year spending total.

These are authorization-stage facts—not invoices, payment records, reimbursement evidence or proof of delivery. CAD is an explicit contextual inference. Column II supplies the supplemental amount; Column I is preserved with its precise accounting basis unverified, not relabeled as an original or current budget.

Recommendation, order/effective date and fiscal-year end remain separate fields. An effective date after the fiscal-year end is reported without declaring the timing unlawful. The Finance Act references are attributed to the official order, not presented as independent current legal interpretation.

Each clause retains exact source tokens, schedule and clause locators, institution label, original order record ID, capture hashes and source links. Order totals are reproduced separately using exact integer arithmetic. Filtered views label fixed whole-order totals so they cannot be mistaken for sums of the visible subset.
