# Question-led authorization brief

The existing, independently reviewed Restructuring Costs annotation now supports a plain-language summary before administrative details. A question above Follow the Money's foundation table opens the filtered 2025–26 authorization view. This is navigation to an existing evidence record, not a newly published case or a claim of misconduct.

The brief appears only when the exact bound target is the sole visible record in its order; unfiltered and multi-record views retain their ordinary full table. It also requires that its annotation has passed review/hash validation. It identifies the line amount, source-dollar currency inference, fiscal year, order, date, stated purpose and unresolved initiative/payee question. “Other government records may contain further detail” remains visible. The line amount is never replaced with the full-order total.

Detailed authorization record expands the original chronology, statutory reference, separately labelled full-order total, compact amount table and source fields. Purpose and amount source-page links are visible in the brief. The annotation review and complete accounting limitations remain available in labelled disclosures. No accepted data, receipt, amount, source hash or coverage count changes.

If the annotation fails verification or another record is selected, the summary is omitted and the verified original authorization information remains available. Regression checks retain municipality journeys and the optional-context failure scenario; browser checks cover question-link/back navigation, short default view, expanded evidence and readable mobile width. No government source requests are required for this presentation change.
