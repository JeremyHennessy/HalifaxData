# A bounded source-reporting investigation

The second public evidence file asks why two rural-municipality operating-balance observations for 2019–20 fail a stated analyst arithmetic test. The exact accepted case is published separately from the foundational budget comparison. Its review is not approval to publish future automated flags.

The test is operating balance before financing/transfers plus financing/transfers minus reported annual balance. Exact values are replayed independently for each source row. The app does not assume that this analyst equation is the publisher’s accounting rule: transfer/sign mapping, original Financial Information Return cells and revision history remain outstanding.

Provincial profiles are separately attributed, rounded annual-operating displays. They are not exact underlying figures to subtract from the portal. Equal and opposite residuals establish neither a transfer nor missing money. Possible alignment, transcription, definition or revision explanations remain unresolved.

Simple, detailed and original-evidence views distinguish confirmed facts, hypotheses, missing records, absent responses and next steps. The chronology labels retrieval dates as retrieval dates. Exact portal IDs, original PDF page links, hashes, review metadata and machine-readable export permit independent checking. No personal blame, waste conclusion or legal allegation is published.
