# Halifax Transit revenue plans and cost measures

`#/transit?view=finance` adds independently reviewed financial context from the same retained annual report as the historical Transit service evidence. It addresses a missing part of the watchdog's money trail: readers can compare the report's planned revenue with its reported actual revenue while keeping service, cost, accounting and payment claims distinct.

This optional context does not replace the unavailable detailed 2026/27 HRM budget. It does not add another collection, change the 17 service observations, or establish a financial ledger. The existing 26 collections and 139 reference identities remain intact.

## What the report establishes

Attachment B printed pages 4–5 / physical PDF pages 37–38 report four actual/planned revenue comparisons for 2024/25:

| Reported scope | Actual source dollars | Planned source dollars | Descriptive actual minus planned |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Conventional | 33,751,558 | 33,400,400 | 351,158 |
| Ferry | 2,803,087 | 2,602,400 | 200,687 |
| Access-A-Bus | 260,349 | 258,000 | 2,349 |
| Halifax Transit overall | 36,814,993 | 36,260,800 | 554,193 |

The overall row overlaps the services. The displayed service actuals sum to 36,814,994, one source dollar more than the reported overall actual. Both are retained, and the published overall amount is used for its own comparison. The plan components exactly reproduce the reported plan total. The unexplained one-dollar display difference is neither silently corrected nor treated as missing money.

These are staff-reported revenue amounts. They do not establish annual expenses, all operating funding, profit, cash receipts, unspent money or a specific Council approval. Currency code and cash/accrual basis remain unestablished; the interface labels source dollars rather than asserting CAD.

## Rounded financial indicators

Printed page 1 / PDF page 34 separately supplies four historical KPI comparisons. Operating expense per passenger is 6.23 then 6.72 source dollars; passenger revenue per passenger is 1.82 then 1.81. Bus & Ferry recovery is 29% then 27%, while All recovery is 27% then 25%. The original reported relative changes are retained, not recalculated from rounded inputs. Bus & Ferry excludes Access-A-Bus; the two recovery scopes overlap.

Printed page 7 / PDF page 40 reports an annual maintenance rate of 1.28 source dollars per kilometre against a budgeted 1.33 and describes it as 3% below budget. The covering report identifies conventional and paratransit vehicles. The projection preserves that percentage and its rounded inputs without asserting that the displayed pair independently reproduces it.

Underlying passenger counts, total expense, detailed cost-recovery methodology and unrounded inputs are unavailable in this projection. No annual spending, subsidy, cash savings, ridership or causal explanation for on-time performance is inferred from these ratios.

## Evidence and release gates

The independently reviewed artifact and receipt each have fixed SHA-256 pins at build and runtime. The context also requires the exact accepted service artifact, its review and the matching directory entry. A finance failure cannot suppress the original service collection or alter its count. A missing or changed parent cannot admit the financial context.

The reviewer inspected retained original PDF pixels, dates, source labels, all amounts and the arithmetic. No new source request was needed. The source URL and retrieval metadata were not independently re-tested. Only numerical facts, short identifiers and original explanation are republished, not charts, PDF pages, signatures or contacts; broad reuse rights are not asserted.

Search, group filters, three reading depths, exact source-page links and JSON export keep the accepted artifact and review intact. Local and hosted browser checks, independent code review and exact deployed-asset verification remain separate from evidence acceptance. No source hold, private history, collector transport or schedule is changed.
