## Conventional-bus reliability

Attachment B printed page 16 / physical PDF page 49 reports annual on-time percentages of 78, 78, 88, 84, 79, 73 and 71 for 2018/19 through 2024/25. The measure covers observed arrivals at designated timepoints (terminals and selected stops), no more than three minutes later than scheduled. The covering report identifies conventional-bus scope. The early-arrival boundary, observation counts, aggregation weights and historical method comparability are not established here. These are rounded reported percentages, not a count of on-time journeys or all stops.

The 2024/25 result of 71% is compared with the stated 85% target. The interface labels 85 - 71 = 14 percentage points as descriptive arithmetic using rounded inputs, not a 14% change, late-trip count, passenger delay or causal finding. The target is not assigned separately to earlier years. No multi-year average or spending-effectiveness comparison is computed.
