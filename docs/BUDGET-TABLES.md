# Captured budget-addendum table subset

Two independently inspected tables from the 70-page 2026–27 budget addendum yield 73 source rows and 219 cells: 213 exact numeric values and six nonnumeric source dashes. The PDF hash and printed/PDF pages are retained in the public evidence metadata; the source PDF itself remains linked rather than copied.

The three columns distinguish the 2025–26 estimate printed in the addendum, its historical 2025–26 forecast and the amended 2026–27 estimate. All monetary cells are CAD thousands. These are not payments, audited actuals or a claim to the latest forecast. Repeated summaries in tables 2.1 and 2.2 are not independent expenditure to add together.

This later captured-PDF review is separate from the earlier foundation’s text-reviewed facts. It does not silently upgrade their provenance. March 10, 2026 remains an announcement date rather than a verified publication date.

The app permits bounded search by row/section, column stage and table. Every cell retains its original token, normalized exact string, financial basis, reporting perimeter, source identity and precise locator. Changed bytes need a new acceptance receipt.
